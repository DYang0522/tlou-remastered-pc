**The Last of Us Remastered: PC port** 0.2.5

Working FSR 3 upscaling and anti-aliasing, keyboard, mouse and Xbox button icons, 2 GB graphics
cards that no longer crash when video memory runs out, about 3 FPS more with fewer hitches and a
little more in busy city streets, around 650 MiB less system memory, a fix for a startup crash on
some NVIDIA cards, and a package that antivirus software no longer flags.

> **No game files are included.** You need your own decrypted copy of the game and its update. This
> project is not affiliated with Sony Interactive Entertainment or Naughty Dog.

**Discord:** help, testing and news: https://discord.gg/9mnjjezcNj

## Fixes and improvements

- **Upscaling and anti-aliasing**: the launcher's **Upscaler** and **Upscaler quality** options did
  nothing. Now the quality presets draw the game below your screen resolution (1.5 to 3 times
  smaller on each side) and FSR 3 upscales it back, with the HUD and subtitles drawn at full
  resolution; at native resolution FSR 3 works as anti-aliasing. Checked on a GTX 1050 Ti at 1080p
  with FSR 3 Performance (960x540 upscaled to 1080p) and FSR 3 native. DLSS takes the same path
  but is not tested yet.
- **Button icons for keyboard, mouse and Xbox controllers**
  ([#171](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/171)): the button prompts in the
  text ("Press ... to ..."), the ones drawn in the world and the menu tabs showed PlayStation
  buttons. With the keyboard and mouse they now show the bound keys and mouse buttons, and with a
  controller that is not a PlayStation one, Xbox-style buttons (A, B, X, Y, LB, RB...). By default
  the icons follow the device you used last; **Button icons** in the launcher's **Controls** window
  (Controller tab) can fix them to keyboard and mouse, PlayStation, Xbox or your controller's own
  (Nintendo letters, for example).
- **Running out of video memory** ([#174](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/174),
  [#177](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/177),
  [#163](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/163),
  [#129](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/129),
  [#71](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/71)): on 2 GB cards (GTX 750 Ti,
  GT 1030), and once on a GTX 1060 at a level load, the game stopped with "out of device memory".
  It now asks for video memory in smaller pieces, frees old textures and tries again, and hands
  back memory it has not used for a few seconds when the card is full, instead of stopping.
  - In the **2-3 GB (saving mode)** video memory choice, shaders saved from earlier sessions load
    when they are first needed instead of all at the start, and a 128 MiB table on NVIDIA cards
    shrinks to under 1 MiB. On a GTX 1050 Ti the game used about 1.5 GB of video memory and about
    4 GB of system memory instead of 2.3 GB and 19.5 GB, at the same frame rate. With only about
    1.4 GB of video memory left free it ran without an error. Not yet tested on a real 2 GB card.
- **Performance**: changes from the God of War III port (SpartanPort) to locking, lookups and frame
  pacing. On a GTX 1050 Ti at 1080p: 44 to 47 FPS in gameplay, shorter worst frames, and the worst
  pause while loading went from about 1.1 s to 0.4 s.
  - In busy city streets the thread that records the draws limits the frame rate. It now does
    less work for each compute fence and no longer checks a debugging list on every draw: about 5%
    less work per draw there. On the GTX 1050 Ti a street that ran at about 37 FPS ran at about 41,
    though part of that difference came from the camera not being in exactly the same place.
- **Less system memory**: about 600 MiB less, from a table that is now built only as it is needed.
- **Startup crash on some NVIDIA cards** (seen on an RTX 2060): the game closed right after the
  Vulkan extension list, at address `00007ff700000000`. A driver query damaged values the game
  kept; they are now saved around it. Not yet tested on the reporter's card; if it still happens,
  turn off **Pipeline binaries** in the launcher.
- **Antivirus false positives** ([#148](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/148)):
  the starters (**TLOU Launcher.exe**, **TLOU Game.exe**) are rebuilt without the C runtime start-up
  code that looked suspicious, and come out byte-for-byte the same in every release. A disassembler
  library file that three engines mistook for a trojan now ships compiled. The whole package scans
  0/68 on VirusTotal, against 3/64 for 0.2.4.
- **Mouse aim while hanging upside down**: in Bill's Town, with Joel caught upside down in the
  snare, the mouse aimed the wrong way. It now follows the camera there too.
- **Walk key**: pressing it while running could stop Joel for a moment before he started walking.
  He now slows from running to walking without stopping.
- **Update executables dumped on a PS5** are now accepted when preparing the game files.
- A diagnostic switch file that Windows refuses to delete no longer stops the game from starting.

## Update

**From 0.1.8 or newer:** use the launcher's **Update** button. **From 0.1.7 or older:** extract the
zip over your previous folder. Your saves, settings and logs are in its `analysis` folder.

## New install

1. Download **TLOU-Remastered-windows.zip** below and extract it to a folder with plenty of free space.
2. Open **TLOU Launcher.exe**.
3. Click **Folders** and choose **Game files**: the folder with `eboot.bin`. Update 1.11 is looked
   for beside it, in the folder of the same name ending in `-patch`.
4. Click **Play**. You can also open **TLOU Game.exe** to start with your saved launcher settings.

Intel integrated graphics (6th generation and newer) need Intel's own current driver for Vulkan
1.3; a laptop maker's older driver can stop at Vulkan 1.2.

## Known issues

- Intel integrated graphics are slow (about 10 FPS at 1080p on a UHD 630).
- Stripes on some walls on RTX cards ([#58](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/58)).
- Volumetric light can look a little odd right at the edges of objects in front of it
  ([#55](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/55)).
- A thin light ring can remain where wheels and feet touch the ground: the game's own design,
  made thinner by **Contact shadows at half resolution**.
- Short pauses can occur the first time an area is played while its shaders compile.
- DLSS and FSR 4 are not in this package: DLSS falls back to FSR 3.
- Dubs from other regions have not been tried with files from a European or Japanese copy yet.
- Online play (Factions) is not available.

When reporting a problem, attach `analysis\tlou\boot.log` (**Open log** in the launcher), or ask
on the Discord server.
